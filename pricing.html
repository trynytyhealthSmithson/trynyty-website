Questions

Who counts as a user?

Each user is one named person with their own sign-in, and each sign-in works on one device at a time. On a team plan, the account owner adds up to five people and can reassign a seat when someone leaves.

How do we pay?

By card at checkout, or by invoice for organizations that need a quote or a purchase order. Ask for an invoice or a quote →

Can we cancel?

Yes. Cancellation stops the next renewal, and access continues through the end of the paid year. We email a reminder before each renewal.

Do you offer custom analysis?

Yes. For analysis built for your market or your contracts, get in touch.
