Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information TRYNYTY health:enablement ("TRYNYTY," "we") collects when you use trynytyhealth.com and the TRYNYTY explorer tools, including the MSSP ACO Explorer, ACO REACH and LEAD Explorer, MA Quality Dashboard, MA Enrollment Explorer, Hospital Financial Explorer and Physician & Practice Explorer (together, the "Services"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Beta sign-ups. When you join a beta, we collect your name, work email, organization, role, the tools you are interested in, how you plan to use them, any feedback you add, and whether you agree to receive emails from us.
  • Feedback and data issue reports. We collect what you write, your name and email if you provide them, and the address and title of the page you were viewing, so we can find what you are reporting.
  • Contact form. When you use the contact form, we collect the information you enter, such as your name, email, organization and message.

Please do not include protected health information or other sensitive personal information about patients in feedback or messages. The Services are built from public CMS data and do not need it.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage analytics. We use Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics to understand how the Services are used, such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, browser and device type, and referring site. Google Analytics uses cookies; Vercel Web Analytics does not.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the time of each request, to operate and secure the Services. When you submit a form, we also store your browser's user agent with the submission.

How we use information

  • To run, maintain and improve the Services, including fixing data issues you report.
  • To respond to your messages and feedback.
  • To contact beta members about the beta, pricing and subscriptions, and, if you agreed, about new features. Every marketing email includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • To understand how the Services are used, in aggregate.
  • To protect the Services against abuse and to comply with law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Services, under their own terms and only for that purpose:

  • Vercel hosts the Services and provides web analytics.
  • Neon stores beta sign-ups, feedback and data issue reports in a database connected through Vercel.
  • Google provides Google Analytics and web fonts.
  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to our email.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of the business, in which case this policy would continue to apply to the information transferred.

How long we keep it

We keep beta sign-ups and feedback for as long as the beta and any resulting subscription relationship continue, and for up to two years after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Analytics data is kept according to the providers' retention settings.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction and deletion. Email andrew@trynytyhealth.com to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Email. You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in the email or by contacting us.
  • Cookies and analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws. We will honor requests as required by applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, such as encrypted connections and access controls at our providers, to protect the information we hold. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Services are intended for healthcare professionals and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change, for example when paid subscriptions begin. The effective date at the top shows when it was last revised. If we make material changes, we will note them on this page and, for beta members, by email.
